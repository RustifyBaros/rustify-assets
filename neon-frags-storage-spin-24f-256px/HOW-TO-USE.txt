Spin frames for neon-frags-storage: 24 frames, 256x256, transparent background.

1. Upload the numbered PNGs somewhere that gives direct image links
   (a GitHub repo, your website, an image host). Keep the file names as they are.
2. In oxide/config/RustifyMenu.json, under the leaderboard prize you want to spin, set:
     "Spin Frame URL ({frame} = 00, 01, ... - blank = no spin)": "https://YOUR-HOST/PATH/neon-frags-storage_{frame}.png"
     "Spin Frame Count": 24
3. Reload the plugin:  oxide.reload RustifyMenu

contact-sheet.png shows every frame over a checkerboard so you can check the edges.
